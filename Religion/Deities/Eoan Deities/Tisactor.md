**Epithet**: God of Technology, Smithing, and Construction
**Symbol**: A Hammer and Anvil, or any other Artisan Tools

## Worshipers

- Smiths
- Artificers
- Engineers
- Artisans More Generally

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Who Needs Tools?**. You may attempt to create without the use of tools. This looks the same, but has summoned or like "remote crafting" with "ghost tools", Suffer -1 die on rolls with these tools or take longer to make things like this
2. **A Small Adjustment**. **Prereq: Who Needs Tools?** You may attempt to alter a work you have completed to be of high quality or have some ability that makes sense (pending GM approbal). However, you must roll for it as if you were making it for the first time (potentially lowering the quality). Additionally, you no longer have disadvantage on working without tools
3. **Immutable Blessing**. **Prereq: A Small Adjustment** You may choose to bless items you create with immutability locking their form, preventing any alteration ( including wear). You may choose to remove this blessing, but it may only be applied to one item at a time

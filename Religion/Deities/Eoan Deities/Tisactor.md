**Epithet**: God of Technology, Smithing, and Construction
**Symbol**: A Hammer and Anvil, or any other Artisan Tools

*"Lord of the forge and all known craft, I call you to guide my hand. Let my creation be strong and match my sight and to myself and all be grand." ~A prayer to [[Religion/Deities/Eoan Deities/Tisactor]].*

## Worshipers

- Smiths
- Artificers
- Engineers
- Artisans More Generally

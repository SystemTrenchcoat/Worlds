The foundation of a world needs to be built with sustainability and purpose in mind, thus is the essence of [[Erashira]]. Represents by [[Arcanaos]], the 7th and final planet from Eotal's sun, [[Erashira]] governs all that makes up the fabric of a world and society. From the laws of mortals and reality, to technology, to stability, [[Erashira]] keeps the wheels of the world turning. She is also associated with planetary, environmental, and self love.

## Epithets

### Kyarisa

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Law and Order

The lawful aspect of [[Erashira]], Kyarisa watches over all that is balance and order. Be it the morality of mortals or the laws of the universe, she makes sure that order is kept, judgement is had, and punishment is set where it is due.

#### [[Divini]]

The universal end of this is [[Divini]], Goddess of Law, Order, and Balance. While she predominantly deals with matters on a cosmic scale, she does also assist in upholding mortal law when called upon. As such, she is worshiped by some law enforcers and powerful magic users who intend to keep balance in the universe.

#### [[Torshana]]

Her mortal-centered counterpart is [[Torshana]], Goddess of Morality, Judgement, and Punishment. She ensures that mortals stay true to their morality and judges them, punishing them should they step out of alignment with them, be it through guilt and shame or something more... intense. She is worshiped by those in the judicial systems of society, ensuring that they are held morally accountable and keep the settlement morally accountable

### Erantei

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Structure and Malleability

The structure and malleability of a world is important to the sustainability and growth of the people. Erantei keeps the realm in a healthy mix of solid and flexible, like clay. 

#### [[Eoterra]]

The physical aspect of this is dominantly embodied by [[Eoterra]], Goddess of [[Eo|Earth]], Stability, and Sustainability. She keeps the realm stable, but still able to sustain itself, allowing room for change. She is worshiped by those who seek stability and sustainability in their lives and settlements.

#### [[Kyasonri]]

Her more metaphysical counterpart is [[Kyasonri]], Deity of Flexibility, Forgiveness, and Compassion. Her energies ensure that the mortals engage in a more compassionate nature, keeping the punishment of [[Torshana]] light in most cases. She also allows further malleability in the they would first appear. She is most worshiped by those seeking more forgiveness, flexibility, or compassion in their lives.

### Sayorasha

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Creation and Destruction

The energies of primal creation, the enrgies that [[Arcaos]] innovates upon, Sayorasha holds the energies of the oldest technologies. She also holds the oldest forms of destruction in volcanoes and similar disasters. Whereas one aspect of her heralds building, the other heralds destruction.

#### [[Tisactor]]

The former is ruled by [[Tisactor]], God of Technology, Smithing, and Construction. Ancient technologies are said to have been inspired by [[Tisactor]] and it is believed he has a sort of chronicle of all the additions and innovations that have come after. He is worshiped by smiths, artificers, engineers, and other artisans throughout the world seeking his knowledge, that they may learn the old ways and perhaps carve their own path.

#### [[Volcune]]

The destructive end of this coin is [[Volcune]], God of Volcanoes, Calamity, and Natural Disasters. They say that destruction is required for creation, that something new be built from the ashes. Perhaps this is why he also has an association with ferility, for the fertile ground the destruction brings. He is worshiped by those insane enough to believe they can control this power and those praying to be spared more than anything. There are few who truly understand the need for his destruction and fewer that worship him for it.

### [[Amaorsel]]

**Symbol**: Arms Around a Heart in a Planet
**Domains**: Love of Environment and Self

The love of the self is fundamental to healthily love anything else. To love the environment and planet one resides on can equally be seen as an expansision of one's self-love. To allow such a love to manifest on such a grand scale, to express such to the lands they call home, this is the love that [[Amaorsel]] watches over; the love of the self, the love of the planet, and the love of the environment.

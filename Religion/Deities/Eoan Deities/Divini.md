Goddess of Law, Order, and Balance. While she predominantly deals with matters on a cosmic scale, she does also assist in upholding mortal law when called upon. As such, she is worshiped by some law enforcers and powerful magic users who intend to keep balance in the universe.

**Epithet**: Goddess of Law, Order, and Balance
**Symbol**: A Scale

## Worshipers

- Law Enforcers
- Powerful Magic Users Who Intend to Keep Balance in the Universe

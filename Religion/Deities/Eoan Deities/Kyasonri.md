**Epithet**: Deity of Flexibility, Forgiveness, and Compassion
**Symbol**: Hands Holding a Clay Heart

Deity of Flexibility, Forgiveness, and Compassion. Her energies ensure that the mortals engage in a more compassionate nature, keeping the punishment of [[Religion/Deities/Eoan Deities/Torshana|Torshana]] light in most cases. She also allows further malleability in the they would first appear. She is most worshiped by those seeking more forgiveness, flexibility, or compassion in their lives.

## Worshipers

- Those Seeking Forgiveness
- Those with Open Hearts
- People, Especially Atheletes, Needing More Flexibility

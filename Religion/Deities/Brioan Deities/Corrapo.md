**Epithet**: God Fungi, Decay, and Rot
**Symbol**: Fungi Sprouting from a Skull

His domain is not a pretty one, but it brings about a renewal, continuing the cycles of life. His domain holds the most curious of creatures, but can be quite dangerous to unassuming souls who attempt to make deals with it or use its power. He is only really worshiped by those who place a heavy importance on life that comes from death, and of course those with a fungi.

## Worshipers

- Those Who Place a Heavy Importance on Life That Comes From Death
- Those With a Fascination of Fungi

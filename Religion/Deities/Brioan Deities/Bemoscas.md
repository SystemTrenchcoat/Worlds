The domain of all things living and natural. From the most beautiful to the most deadly disease, the most majestic beast to the most unassuming fungus, from the change in seasons to the change in weather, [[Bemoscas]] rules it all. Represented in the sky as [[Tunari]], the 6th planet from the sun, [[Bemoscas]] rules all natural beings and natural phenomona. He also watches over the love of pets, plants, and nature itself.

## Epithet

### Karana

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Flora and Fauna

What would nature be without flora and fauna, the seasons, the beauty and wildness of the natural world. All of this is watched over by Karana. She tends to the needs of the plants and animals of [[Eotal]], the cycles of seasons and preserving life and beauty. 

#### [[Fiersa]]

The plants are watched by the aspect [[Fiersa]], Goddess of Flora, Sleep, and Beauty. Plants are some of the simplest, yet completely beautiful things in nature. So many have the ability to defend themselves through thorns, poison, or even sleep. Sleep has its own beauty to it to, a softness and peace one rarely exhibits while awake. She mostly worshiped by druids, herbalists, gardeners, some artists, and insomniacs.

#### [[Diasi]]

==Animals are ruled by [[Diasi]], Goddess of Fauna, Hunting, and Wilderness. Fauna have their own beauty, but at its core, it is a wild one. Cycles like the seasons, driven by minds and desires of their own, they can be some of the most wild and unpredictable creatures. He is most worshiped by farmers, druid, survivalists, and hunters.==

### Tuvasa

Nature has its own order, its own law, its own right and wrong. That said, when mortals started farming, new laws and customs were, in a sense, made. The sanctity of life was still something to be respected, but mortals still forged their own ecosystems in their agriculture.

#### [[Briosa]]

The former is watched over by [[Briosa]], Goddess of Nature, [[Brio|Life]], and Seasons The natural world is protected by her and her followers, the very essence of life in her domain. She is mostly worshiped by guardians of nature, mostly druids, as well as many naturalists, and nature based guilds like the Children and Ambassadors of Nature.

#### [[Fruila]]

Her more mortal-influenced counterpart is [[Fruila]], Goddess of Weather, Crops, and Agriculture. She is also associated with rivers for the benefits they have in farms. She watches over the entire farm, influencing and ensuring a balanced ecosystem. She also rules over weather, bringing rain, snow, fog, and drought wherever necessary. She is most worshiped by farmers, but also by those seeking to change or predict the weather

### Cyrasyona

**Symbol**: A Dark, Splochy Rot
**Domains**: 

Not all life is pretty to look at or even safe to be near. Rot, blight, disease, fungi, all life that sits on the bridge of death are the creatures of Cyrasyona's domain. 

#### [[Corrapo]]

On the more benign end is [[Corrapo]], God Fungi, Decay, and Rot. His domain is not a pretty one, but it brings about a renewal, continuing the cycles of life. His domain holds the most curious of creatures, but can be quite dangerous to unassuming souls who attempt to make deals with it or use its power. He is only really worshiped by those who place a heavy importance on life that comes from death, and of course those with a fungi.

#### [[Dilitrio]]

His more malignent counterpart is [[Dilitrio]], God of Disease, Plague, and Famine. He heralds forces that consume life and leave only decay and destruction in return. He has very few followers, only the most derranged and psychotic would follow him, though it is custom in some areas to pray to him during an epidimic that you, your loved ones, and your home may be spared.

### [[Amaoria]]

The love an owner has for a pet, that a gardener has for a plant, the love an artificer has for their automaton, three different, yet similar scenarios that [[Amaoria]] watches over. The love of a pet for some is quite a lot like a plant for another, a thing that is loved and cared for, that one can cultivate and watch grow, that one can pour love into it they so desire. Anything that is loved and cared for as one would a pet is watched over by [[Amaoria]]. Her love is that of creatures and beings not as intelligent as mortals, but are loved and cared for by them. Arguably, even the love of a child could fit under her love, at least until they have a deeper understanding of existence.

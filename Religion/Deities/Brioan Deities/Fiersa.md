**Epithet**: Goddess of Flora, Sleep, and Beauty
**Symbol**: An Intricate Flower, Sometimes with a Sleeping Woman

*"Guardian and grower of all plants, holder of their secrets, we look upon you to make our gardens grow. Guide us to our rooted kin, let us raise them and keep them, let us draw upon their power and use it as our own." ~A prayer to [[Fiersa]].*

## Worshipers

- Druids
- Herbalists
- Gardeners
- Some Artists
- Insomniacs

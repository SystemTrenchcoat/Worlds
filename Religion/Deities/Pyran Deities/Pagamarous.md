God of War, Storms, and Destruction. War is an art to  [[Pagamarous]] and he needs little reason to engage in it. He revels in the destruction, the death and chaos it brings. He also rules over storm for the storm brings about just as much destruction as war. Some might say it is like the sky going to war with the land. He is almost exclusively worshiped by war-mongers and brutes.
**Epithet**: God of War, Storms, and Destruction
**Symbol**: Storm Cloud with a Lightning Bolt out the Bottom

## Worshipers

- War-Mongers
- Brutes
- Those Who Crave Violence

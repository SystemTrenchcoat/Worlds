**Epithet**: Goddess of [[Pyr|Fire]], Strength, and Passion
**Symbol**: 3 Tiered Flame

## Worshipers

- Those Seeking Strength
- Those Seeking the Light and Warmth of Flame to Fuel Them

## TTRPG Abilities

1. Pool of 6d6s to be given to aid a roll
2. Die increases to d8s. Additionally, they can be subtracted from a roll instead
3. Die increases to d12s. Additionally, 1 is restored on a successful contested roll (from use, unaided by Pyra's abilities)

**Epithet**: Goddess of Luck, Gambling, and Fortune
**Symbol**: Playing Cards, Usually a Three Card Hand

## Worshipers

- Gamblers
- Game Masters
- People Seeking Luck

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Fortune's Favor**. Luck always seems to be on your side. Roll luck based rolls with Fate's Favor. You may also roll with Fate's Favor on any one roll once a day.
2. **Games of Chance**. Your game habits have granted you special abilities. Choose dice, cards, or some other tool/game of chance. Each card, roll, etc. has a different effect and calling the effect enhances its efficacy. You can summon this tool/game any time. You may use a provided game set or make your own with the "**Games of Chance Effects**" table, each with 1 of 4 categories. Your table should have as close to equal parts each category as possible.
3. **Enhanced Effect**. **Prereq: Games of Chance** Your **Games of Chance** skill has gotten stronger. Upgrade the effects to match the provided list or create a new list utilizing the "**Enhanced Effects**" Table and the same rules as **Games of Chance**

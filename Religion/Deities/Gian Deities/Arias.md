**Epithet**: Goddess of Creativity, Music, and the Arts
**Symbol**: Usually an Artist's Palette, but Any Tool Used to Make Art

## Worshipers

- Artists
- Musicians
- Creatives more generally

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **A Thousand Words**. Your proficiency with your craft allows you to convey deeper meaning through your art. You may use your art/performance checks instead of convey(persuassion) given adequate time
2. **The Show Must Go On**. The world around you shifts slightly, allowing you to make your stage anywhere. No matter the medium, you are able to perform any art form you have experience with, the world shifting around you to make any accomadations, including amplifying speed or creating equipment. All equipment and supplies created this way disappear. Increased speed drains all the energy required to perform the task nornally. Pushing past your natural limits causes the **[Minor Metaphysical Harm](Harm)** "exhaustion" (gives you one level of exhaustion)
3. **Immersive Works**. Reality twists further around your art. During a performance, you may create a number of incoporeal actors that can assist you through the performance. They can manipulate light objects and move within your line of sight. They can also inhabit physical objects no larger than a doll

**Epithet**: God of Science, Medicine, and Research
**Symbol**: A Beaker of Any Kind (Usually the Triangular One)

## Worshipers
- Researchers
- Scientists
- Medical Practitioners

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Researcher's Insight**. When you roll **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Will** to do research, increase the Level of Success by 1
2. **Pelarius's Guidance**. When you would utilize your past research, the information flows through you, guiding your actions, appearing as a visual overal or something similar. Gain +1 LoS when utilizing resarch.
3. **Scientist's Wisdom**. You may attempt to research any subject through meditation. Decrease the LoS by 2, but on a success, you learn of a useful source, the contents of such source, or witness a useful experiment during your meditation (GM's disgression which) (must last at least 10 minutes)

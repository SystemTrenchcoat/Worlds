The domain of consciousness, spirit, and all things death and rebirth, it is represented by [[Yusoria]], the second planet from the sun. His domain is that of knowledge, awareness, and the immortal soul. His most worshiped face is that which speaks of death and memory, reminding of the impermanence of their time and the importance of remembering what is important. Behind that lies the face of conscious awareness and knowledge, teaching introspection and connection to one's bloodline. Last, but far from least, life after death and all of the intricacies that come with it. He also embodies familial love, reminding all to remember where they came from.

## Epithets

### Caparicus

**Symbol**: Graveyard
**Domains**: Death and Memorial

The face of mortality and a reminder of the afterlife, Caparicus watches over death, the afterlife and memory, especially of those passed. Caparicus reminds people that they will live and then die, becoming a memory on the physical and something else in the afterlife. He also rules over memory, of the dead, of the living, of the mundane, of the chaotic, of the exciting, reminding us to cherish these memories and embrace them with love and excitement, after all, they are what make life worth living. 

#### [[Eido]]

The face of the former is [[Eido]], God of Death, The Dead, and The Afterlife. [[Eido]] watches over the dead, making sure they find their way to the afterlife. He is worshiped by undertakers, those who practice funerary rites, exorcist, mediums, and those seeking swift or painless end. 

#### [[Animus]]

His more lively counterpart is [[Animus]], Deity of Mortality, Memory, and Remembrance. If there was an entity or deity whose motto is "memento mori", it would be [[Animus]]. He reminds people that one day, they will die, and so they should make the most of it, holding the good memories and learning from the bed. Additionally, they watch over the memory of the dead, after all, the dead living on in the physical through memory. They are also worshiped by those who practice funerary rites, as well as thrill-seekers, those who lice life on the edge, hypnotists, and those who work with memory.

### Inaserai

**Symbol**: An eye piercing a veil
**Domains**: Self and Lineage

Beyond the physical of reality, we have the consciousness that pervades everything. Looking inwards we find ourselves, our lineage, our habits, and open the doorway to new understanding. Inaserai promotes introspection not always embraced by mortals, but allows for a far deeper understanding of themselves and creation.

#### [[Anio]]

The first face of this is [[Anio]], Deity of [[Ani|Spirit]], Consciousness and The Unconscious Self. Anio rules over the element of spirit and all of the intricacies of perception and extra-dimensional interaction that entails. Anio rules over the self, conscious and unconscious, physical and non-physical. They act as a guide to the journey into the self, teaching one's habits to them so they may rewrite them. He is worshiped by those who seek this inner work: monks, mystics, and enlightened thinkers to name a few.

#### [[Hemerrah]]

The second face of this is known as [[Hemerrah]], Goddess of Blood, Knowledge, and Lineage. [[Hemerrah]] guides souls to follow their roots to their family tree to find the knowledge it hides. She also guides those who wish to assist in this work (consistently or not). She is worshiped by those who seek to learn about their lineage, who help to guide others to do so, or seek to learn about others through their blood.

### Icarno

**Symbol**: Skeletal Phoenix
**Domains**: Life Beyond Death

The face of one's next life. There is more than one path for what happens when we die. There is, of course, the standard afterlife, a realm of memories, or a rebirth in another plane, but there is also reincarnation and undeath. Some believe there is such a thing as immortality, especially seeing as many of [[The Initiates]] are still around from the beginning of mortal civilization, though few have a clue where to start beyond rebirth and undeath.

#### [[Metageasi]]

The domain of the former is ruled by [[Metageasi]], God of Reincarnation, Rebirth, and god-touched. The first two are self explanatory, but the last refers to those favored by gods and granted the gift of immortality, as well as demigods. It is written in lowercase because it is only known to a select few mortals. He is worshiped by those seeking to live again in the physical plane in a new flesh, sometimes in dying breaths, but often long before.

#### [[Necrotori]]

[[Necrotori]], in contrast, is the Goddess of Undeath, Vengeance, and Unfinished Business. She rules over the undead and every way of becoming one. She feeds on the desire to live forever, by any means, the rage and hate of those unjustly or brutally killed, and the need to fulfill a purpose of those taken before they could accomplish their highest ambitions. She is worshiped by the undead and necromancers, and is hated by [[Eido]] for her crimes against death.

### [[Amaosa]]

**Symbol**: A Heart with a {/{Soul/ A wispy near teardrop}/} in the Middle
**Domains**: Family and Familial Bonds

The Goddess of Familial Love, Ancestors, and Found Family. She reminds us that our love is beyond blood, it permeates our very essence. Those who we see as family, who we love as family, are our family. She connects us to them through that love, as well as to our ancestors. Our ancestors are said to watch over us and this is facilitated through their love for us and our love for them. Familial love connects us to our closest companions, be it blood family or soul family, it is all met with the same love and appreciation

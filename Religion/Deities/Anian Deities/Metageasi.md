The ascended deity of ascendance. Born of divine blood, ascended by divine battle, he now embodies a path of transcendence. Additionally, he is seen as a guardian to those with divine connection, protecting them from divine connection, protecting them from divines. He is dutiful, responsible, and inspirational to near all. He near solely takes on the form of a strapping, muscular [[ion-kin]] with ashen skin with black and glowing gold tattoos across his body, short and spiky black hair, and swirling raspberry and amethyst eyes.

**Epithet**: God of Reincarnation, Rebirth, and god-touched
**Symbol**: Chrysalis with a Body on the Front

### Worshipers
- Those seeking to live again in the physical plane in a new flesh, sometimes in dying breaths, but often long before.

The energies of emotion and fluidity are quite powerful in their own right. Emotions have the power to fuel someone unlike anything else. They can lead us to our goals, desires, and ambitions, or of spirals of hate, coldness, and destruction. It's all about what we allow, how we allow, if we flow or resist, if we listen to the current or push against it with all our might. Our ability to surrender can bring forth guidance and new perspectives, while resistance can leave only fear in its place. Represented by the moon, or as it is called no Eotal: [[Lorae]], [[Elfuelo]] heralds the ever-changing, fluid world of emotions and flow. He also represents romantic love, affection, and attraction.

## Epithets

### Vioco

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Emotion and Flow

The realm of emotions is one heavily dictated by flow. Move with the emotions, the events, the present, lice a tranquil life. Pull against what is true inside and face only misery and strife. While some circumstances can influence the result, ultimately, the resistance to the flow is what brings about the pain.

#### [[Viernei]]

The flow is governed by [[Viernei]], God of Tranquility, Flow, and Appreciation. He heralds the benefits of allowing, of flowing, of being present and adaptive to life. Tranquility comes from knowing that releasing anything painful is a matter of feeling and releasing, and from this, an appreciation for the present can be born. He is most worshiped by some monks and those seeking more of this release in their lives.

#### [[Trisella]]

His restrictive counterpart is [[Trisella]], Goddess of Misery, Grief, and Strife. She heralds what happens when gone can not accept what has happened. When one gets trapped in a moment of circumstance, they repeat it, over and over until they can accept it and flow once more. She is most worshiped by those in mourning to help them through.

### Cyovano

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Intuition and Waterways

The water is a lot like emotion: flowing, collecting to overflowing when damned up, and when allowed to flow, it can guide us. It is well believed that emotions guide us to what we truly desire. These inner messages are known as our intuition, our inner guide. Beyond the personal, Cyovano also watches over all waterways from the vast oceans to the smallest rivers. 

#### [[Nera]]

The personal aspect is known as [[Nera]], Goddess of Cold, [[Ner|Water]], and Intuition. When one detaches and allows, they can grow somewhat cold, though it is believed that this coldness comes with a strengthened intuition, a stronger connection with inner knowledge. They are worshiped by certain mystics that wish to strengthen their intuition.

#### [[Aeoqor]]

The physical aspect is known as [[Aeoqor]], God of [[Lorae|the Moon]], Waterways, and Revealed Secrets. [[Lorae|The moon]]'s affect on the tide is well known in most of [[Eotal]], as is its ability to reveal the true forms of shape-changers. [[Lorae|The moon]] is known to reveal truths in secrets and guide not only the waters, but the people who call to it for guidance. [[Aeoqor]] is mostly worshiped by seafaring folk, lycanthropes, and those seeking to see beyond illusion.

### Mesnara

**Symbol**: 
**Domains**: Night and Guidance

The intuition is brought out most in the night. Be it through the silence, dreams, or welcoming the unknown, the flow of energy offers much to willing recipients. Dreams can guide through the stories and experiences they share. The night can allow one to observe the unknown if they can steel themselves enough to surrender to it.

#### [[Somera]]

The former is ruled by [[Somera]], Goddess of Dreams, Silence, and Guidance. The link between intuition and the dream-state is well-known in [[Eotal]], seen as clever ways to teach oneself lessons the need to learn. She is most worshiped by those seeking dream guidance, some mystics, and some monks.

#### [[Nyct]]

The latter is ruled by [[Nyct]], Deity of Night, Surrender, and the Unknown. The night holds many secrets, invisible to the fearful eye. Only through surrender can one truly come to learn the mysteries obscured by the night. He is worshiped by those seeking the knowledge and power of night, some monks, and some mystics.

### [[Amaora]]

**Symbol**: A Kiss in a Heart
**Domains**: Romance and Affection

Romantic love, attraction, and affection are the tenets of her domain. [[Amaora]] is seen as the feeling one might call "butterflies in your stomach".They herald deep, soft love, the type where you listen to each other and cherish every word, every moment. Her soft touch strikes the heart of all she flows through, spreading the softest love one could imagine.

**Epithet**: God of Tranquility, Flow, and Appreciation
**Symbol**: A Falling Feather

## Worshipers

- Some Monks
- Those Seeking Release

## TTRPG Abilities

1. You have a pool of 5d6s that can be used to enhance a roll. These dice are refilled after a rest
2. You may expend one to increase your **[[Stance]]** or **[[Result]]** in a situation
3. You may ask a question from the perspective of your character. The GM must give truthful advice, but specifics are up to them.

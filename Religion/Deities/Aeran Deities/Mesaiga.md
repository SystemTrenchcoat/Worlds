The energies of thought and change swirling and changing in the wind. Our thoughts and ideas are what bind us to each other, what forges civilizations, language, and friendships. Just as much as it is known for community and comradry, it is known for deceit, conquest, and cunning. All those who would use ideas for their own selfish gains, truth or no. However, it is also an energy of travel, promises, trade, and protection. All things in transit are kept by [[Mesaiga]], and promises and protection are build upon our connections. [[Mesaiga]] is represented in the stars as [[Menartia]], the fifth planet from the sun. He aslo represents platonic love and friendships, the bonds we make throughout our lives.

## Epithets

### Cyertow

**Symbol**: A Tablet of Laws
**Domains**: Society and Bonds

The society and its promises of protection are the corner stones to all races of intelligent creature. The information shared, the communications had, the agreements of a people were what bound them. Their codes were their law, the keys to what they viewed themselves. Their promises to themselves and their ability to communicate with one another not only alloweed for survival, but an ability to form relationships within their communities, to form new ones.

#### [[Tazo]]

[[Tazo]] is the face of the foundation, God of Promises, Language, and Protection, some of the tenets of a poweful civilization. To promise is to bind protection is one of the biggest promises of living in a group. He is most worshiped by guardians, writers, linguists, and some diplomats.

#### [[Isagrei]]

His counterpart is the benefits these promises and foundation can make, [[Isagrei]], God of Society, Information, and Communication. Arguably, communication is equally essential as promise, especially considering one can be seen as a subsection of the other. However, while the promises lay the ground work, communication and information are the vital fluid, keeping a society together. He is often worshiped by law makers, teachers, and communicators, as well as those seeking guidance, be it to forge their own society or for a certain piece of information. The [[Lyceum]] quite favors this god

### Pyer

**Symbol**: A City Skyline
**Domains**: 

As society gets bigger and more advanced, new concepts start to flow through like gusts of wind. Ideas of travel, commerce, trade, and new concepts of what should be bringing society into a new era. Not quite innovation, after all, innocation has a starting point, those are all new ideas, never thought in the primative society. A great change as an era shifts, as an era shifts, as a great many new roles are created, a great any disciplines conceived of and learned.

#### [[Aeris]]

The aspect of this shift is [[Aeris]], Goddess of [[Aer|Air]], Change, and Ideas. Her energy flows through settlements of a certain intelligence, those willing to adapt and create new things, conceive of new thoughts. She is worshiped by revolutionaries, inventors, and philosophers and those who take on change as a part of them (like changelings).

#### [[Mercantur]]

The aspect of the more specific advancements is [[Mercantur]], God of Travel, Commerce, and Trade. He represents the ideas that sweep through a great many of the settlements of the world. He is worshiped by merchants, tradesmen, and travellers.

### Nyerator

**Symbol**: A Hazy Smiling Face
**Domains**: 

The air of change brings with it an air of deceit. This conquest and cunning. Thieves, bandits, brotherhoods of assassins, liars, cheaters, side-steppers of the law are all bound by this aspect.

#### [[Erimos]]

The former 3 worship [[Erimos]], God of Conquest, Comradory, and Thieves. He brings together those who work together in the shadows for the name of conquering. 

#### [[Caliel]]

The latter 3 worship [[Caliel]], God of Deceit, Cunning, and Lies. The deceivers of the world shift like the sands in the wind, using the same feels that bring together to destroy or chase their selfish ends.

### [[Amaoris]]

**Symbol**: A Handshake in a Heart
**Domains**: 

God of friendship, platonic love, and business partners, he watches over things sared amongst people. The tender moments of friendship, the memories of fun times together, the resources shared between people. He heralds connection to those of similar interests and ideals and brings people together in their willingness to explore and connect. Friends can be found in the oddest of places in the most unexpected of ways, and yet those friendships can last lifetimes upon lifetimes.

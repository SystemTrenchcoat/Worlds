**Epithet**: God of Society, Information, and Communication
**Symbol**: A Tower

## Worshipers

- Law Makers
- Teachers
- Communicators
- Those Seeking Guidance

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Political Power**. Gain +1d8 to rolls that position you as the leader and +2d4 to rolls made for decisions/actions made as a group
2. **Societal Support**. Increase "Autocrat Die" to 1d12 and on a success, increase the **[[Stance]]** by 1 when you are poised as the leader. Increase "Democracy Pool"  to 3d4 and gain +1 **[[Result]]** on these rolls
3. **Will of the People**. Increase "Democracy Pool" to 5d4 and gain 1 "Good Will" when you succeed a task which can be used to improve NPC's view of the party (must explain how it relates). Increase "Autocrat Die" to 1d20 and gain "Good Will", same as above, except it can only be used by you and needn't be "good"

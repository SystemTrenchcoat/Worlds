**Epithet**: Deity of Insanity, Obsession, and Negativity
**Symbol**: A Cheshire Grin, Sometimes Laughing

## Worshipers

- Those All Alone
- Those Unhealthily Driven
- Those Completely Consumed by Darkness/Greed
- Fanatics
- Seekers of Forbidden Truths
- Purveyors of Darkness, Dread, and Fear

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Forbidden Knowledge**. You long for knowledge that most would rather keep buried. Choose the topic of your curiosity from the "Forbidden Knowledge" table. Gain +2d4 on rolls relating to this topic, but remember, many would grow weary of you if they learned of it.
2. **Obsessive Habits**. **Prereq: Forbidden Knowledge** You're getting closer, much closer, to finding the apex of your research. Increase the bonus from "Forbidden Knowledge" to 3d4, but suffer -1 **[[Result]]** on **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Will** rolls not relating to your "muse".
3. **Pariah's Secrets**. **Prereq: Obsessive Habits** You have found the height of the knowledge you sought, but it changed you. Gain the "secret" under your "forbidden knowledge",and suffer -1 **[[Stance]]** in Social interactions as the effects of your research is written across your body

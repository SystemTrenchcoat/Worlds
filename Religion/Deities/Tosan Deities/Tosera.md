**Epithet**: Goddess of [[Tos|Light]], Truth and Positivity
**Symbol**: Light Shining Through a Magnifying Glass

## Worshipers

- Truth-Tellers
- Beloved Leaders
- Those With a Strong or Positive Force of Personality

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Inspirational Words**. Pool of 4d6s to offer to allies or self following saying something inspiring
2. **Actions over Words**. You can spend 2 dice to allow an ally to push through harm. Regain 1 die when you succeed a roll affected by [[Harm]].
3. **Overcoming Odds**. You may spend 3 dice to give you or an ally Fate's Favor on a roll following saying or doing something inspiring.

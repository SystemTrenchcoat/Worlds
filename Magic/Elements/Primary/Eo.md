Stability consistency, malleability, how one flows and adapts to change (bend, stay, break, etc.), how one deals with pressure, structure, physically, force of functionality

The structure of all things, embodying laws, restraints, etc. creating a foundation upon which all is built atop, micro and macro scale.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Sum of its Parts**. You may roll **Recall** or **Diligence** to determine the composition of something you can see or otherwise sense
2. **Reconfigure**. If you know the composition of something, you may roll a **Craft**, **Recall**, **Diligence**, **Will**, or **Spellcraft** check to attempt to alter it (removing parts, adding some from elsewhere, etc.). This only works on non-living things
3. **Foundational Knowledge**. You have grown accustomed to the nature of things and can often determine something's foundation. Make a **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Will** roll. On a success, you determine a foundational piece of info on a target, be it an origin or weakness.

### Elemental Manipulation

1. **Minor Eo Manipulation**. You can slightly alter dirt, sand, and dust through magical means, dispersing, packing, or molding them
2. **Moderate Eo Manipulation**. **Prereq: Minor Eo Manipulation** Your Eo Manipulation extends to gravel, rocks, and clay, adding the ability to break medium rocks into gravel, gravel into sand or dust, or compact them back
3. **Major Eo Manipulation**. **Prereq: Moderate Eo Manipulation** You fundamentally undersatnd how Eo exists, how it ebbs and flows through the world. You can manipulate this element in non-living things
4. **Masterful Eo Manipulation**. **Prereq: Major Eo Manipulation** Your Eo Manipulation now extends to living things as well

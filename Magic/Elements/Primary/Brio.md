Ability to live and grow organically, desire to adapt and expand, size, instincts, connection to nature, perseverence, tenacity, nature vs. nurture, force of livliness

One's ability to grow, adapt, and evolve. Strong brio indicates a natural tendency to adapt and evolve, while weak brio indicates struggles or further work to do so.

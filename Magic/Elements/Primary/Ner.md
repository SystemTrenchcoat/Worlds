## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Energetic Affinity**. You have the ability to sense the powers affecting something. At a glance, you can determine one power affecting a person, object, etc. You may roll a Difficulty [TBD] **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Will** roll to determine another affinity, the level of its influence (number of prints allocated), or a specific capability of it (one of the power granted abilities). The difficulty increases by 5 with each additional use on a given target.
2. **Energy Manipulation**. Your understanding of how energy moves grants you the ability to change its flow. You may allow a creature to reallocate a point in one of their power skill trees.
3. **Energy Transmutation**. **Prereq: Energy Manipulation** You have learned the secret of transforming one energy into another. You may allow a creature to reallocate a point in their power skill trees. It may move across skill trees. You may also attempt to pull another's energy to you, weakening them while bolsetering yourself. Roll opposing Magic checks. If you are the victor, you steal a point from a power you are aware they possess. This removes the highest level ability in that tree. If there are multiple, the GM decides. This point may be used by anyone you choose. On a fail, they are immune to this ability for a full day.

4. **Minor Ner Manipulation**. You have a connection to Ner, the element of water and energy. You can control about a liter of water you can see. Water controlled in this waty will move as you will it (movement and shapes), but still otherwise obey reality.
5. **Moderate Ner Manipulation**. **Prereq: Minor Ner Manipulation** Your connection to Ner has grown, granting further ability to control it. You can control up to 10 liters of water you can see.
6. **Major Ner Manipulation**. **Prereq: Moderate Ner Manipulation** Your connection to Ner grows stronger, granting significant control of it. You can control up to 200 liters of water. You may attempt to surpass this limit with a Magic roll.
7. **Masterful Ner Manipulation**. **Prereq: Major Ner Manipulation** You fundamentally understand how Ner ebbs and flows in the world. You can manipulate this element in all things

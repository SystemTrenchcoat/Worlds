Liquid with magical properties, be it increased viscosity, acidity, etc. It is, and created, the first entities who were separate of their creator, as basic as they are

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Minor Fluid Alteration**. You are able to adjust the mundane, benign properties of fluids (viscosity, density, odor, color, etc.)
2. **Moderate Fluid Alteration**. **Prereq: Minor Fluid Alteration** You are able to adjust more dangerous mundane properties of fluids (pH, reactability, flamibility)
3. **Major Fluid Alteration**. **Prereq: Moderate Fluid Alteration** You are able to add unique properties to fluids (such as poison/venom, physical autonomy, etc.)

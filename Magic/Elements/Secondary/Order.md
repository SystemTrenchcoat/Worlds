The force that gives all things its place, arbitrating the laws of all that exists and will ever exist, if differently across circumstances.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Orderly Proficiency**. +2 per die (min +2) on rolls to maintain order.
2. **Average Aptitude**. You may forgo any toll and take the average instead.
3. **Order Over Chaos**. You may roll a Difficulty 38 Magic (**Spellcraft**) check when you would instead roll on a random table. On a success, you choose the result. On a fail, the GM chooses the result however they see fit.

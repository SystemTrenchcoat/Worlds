The most basic form of change, allowing for the old to give way to the new. It is the force that gives space for will and its creativity.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Chaos Surge**. You may roll on one of the "chaos tables" at your disgression. The GM may force you to roll at theirs. If a roll occurs more than once in a row, a chaotic surge occurs setting off a GM determined effect and dealing the **[Minor Metaphysical Harm](Harm)** "Chaos-Touched".
2. **Chosen Chaos**. You and the GM may change the chaos table between sections. Sample entries are shown in "Chaos Entries", each with 1 of 4 categories. You must have equal parts (or as close to equal as the die will allow). The table should prioritize chaos over all else
3. **Controlled Chaos**. You may roll on the table twice, taking the preferred result

That in between the very fabric of creation. The very essence of the void, the matter of the cosmos, the space between self and thought, mind and emotion. It is, in essence, emptiness, space that is required to be.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Focus**. You may enter a state where the outside world seems to fade away. Gain Fate's Favor on **Craft**, **Adaptability**, and **Recall** checks, but suffer Disadvantage (will eventually be renamed) on **Social**, **Agility**, and any other checks that deal with your surroundings
2. **Liminal Pulse**. **Prereq: Focus** You may extend your Focus state to another
3. **Creative Origin**. **Prereq: Focus** You may temporarily create an object you are familiar with from thought alone.

The foundation of knowledge. Thought to be the essence of intelligent language, it portrays entire ideas in concise symbols. While much material magic was born of their knowledge, it is basic compared to its capabilities.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Glyph Basics**. You can read the intention of any material written language. You may attempt to read planar languages with a **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Will** roll
2. **Linguistic Origins**. **Prereq: Glyph Basics** Your familiarity with glyphs allows further insight when reading. You can determine scripts at a glance and may roll a **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Will** or Social (some int or cha) roll to determine more info on the writer. Additionally, you may read planar languages' intention with Glyph Basics.
3. **Creation Glyphs**. **Prereq: Linguistic Origins** Your connection to Glyphs has revealed complex symbols only you seem able to see. You may see and read Creation Runes (glyphs that govern reality itself). Additionally, you may inscribe Lesser Creation Runes. You may only have one active.
    - Require a Magic Roll to determine strength
    - May not override another Creation Rune
    - Cannot be overridden while they exist

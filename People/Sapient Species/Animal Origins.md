
Locusts originated in [[Infernis|The Hells]] and have a destruction, carnage, chaos, and hunger variety

Butterflies and bees also originated in [[Infernis|The Hells]], but may have reached the heavens through [[Beauty Crossroads]], where beauty lands create a mirror or portal to the heavens

Beetles are considered creatures of rebirth, renewal, immortality, resilience, and resourcefulness

Ants were infernals like locusts with the same varieties, same with wasps and bees

Mantises were bug celestials bound by "survival of the fittest"

[[Infernis|The Hells]] have an almost solid world, it is comparible to the material. [[Ceylium|The Heavens]] in contrast are more multidimensional, making it tricky to percieve (but still possible)

Dragonflies, like lady bugs, are bugs of beauty. They originated in the hells but flowed to [[Ceylium|The Heavens]]

Birds of Prey are survival of the fittest celestials with gym-bro mentalities, the full spectrum of them

Frogs are infernals most associated with hunger, chaos, and change

Lizards are celestials of survival of the fittest. Occassionally, they would flow to the hells at a [[Beauty Crossroads|Beauty Crossroad]]

Crickets and songbirds are creatures of beauty. They exist in both [[Ceylium|The Heavens]] and [[Infernis|The Hells]]

All bugs have a greater presence in [[Infernis|The Hells]], though there are those that come higher through [[Beauty Crossroads]]. They often take the Natural Order or Survival of the Fittest, some protection, especially across smaller/weaker species. These protectors are usually larger versions with more defensive capability. Many insects end up flowing to [[Ceylium|The Heavens]] eventually, most a few centuries befor [[Kyarus]]. Some bugs, like ants and bees, who are very ordered naturally may have had a celestial counterpart come about at the time of such a development

Butterflies didn't originally have patterns, they used smell, it was gained through the angelic replication affect of [[Beauty Crossroads|Beauty Gateways]]. They gained a pattern that changes somewhat depending on the flowers they have flown to and their energies.

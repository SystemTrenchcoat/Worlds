Beao are mortals that evolved from creatures before The [[Age of Intelligent Beasts]]. Despite having bestial ancestors, the wave of energy that created the [[mortals]] mutated the first beao, making them more mortal than beast. Thanks to this lineage, many beao societies prioritize these animal instincts and allow them to shape their tribes and cities alike. Some prioritize the hunt, for instance, as a rite of passage, a vital skill to be learned to pay homage to their lineage and people, while others see such as primitive and barbaric, especially as beao grew more entwined with the other mortals. 

There are as many kinds of beao as there are kinds of animal, but what connects them all is their direct ties to animals. Some known subspecies of beao include:

- [[Feili]]
- [[Dreit]]
- [[Scorpi]]

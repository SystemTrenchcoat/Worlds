
There are a great many creatures who lurk on the outskirts of what we would call reality. [[Demons]], [[Angels]], [[Fae]], [[Spirits]], [[Elementals]], all manor of creature our mortal minds would see has fantastical, or perhaps horrifying. It is always difficult to tell with such things, mortals are so different from one another, each with their differing experiences. Shaped by them, molded by them, until they wish to become something more.

Some mortals long to be like those other creatures. Perhaps in cosmetic ways with halos, tails, and horns. Perhaps in more helpful ways with extra arms, eyes, ears, even wings. Perhaps in more eccentric ways, guided by the whispers of the creatures, the beings. Such a funny word, being, Noun... Verb... A creature or an action. How would one look at this further with the help of another being so different from themselves

How would a human change if the were "being" more like a celestial/angel? Would they act only on good and light? Would they glow bright enough to fill a room? Ever watching. Ever protecting against chaos. Ever loving in a way never thought possible for a human.

How about a fae? Famously different from humans in so many ways... Their morality would change first I believes, caring more for nature's soverignty and wellbeing far more than man or society, especially society. Would they benevolently guide other men like sheep, or would they raze the society like it did to the natural world? Would they still look human in any way but frame? Vines streaking through their hair, eyes of a predator, feathers coating what once was skin, claws pressing out from their bloody branch-like fingers?

How about an elemental? There are so many kinds; fire, water, air. earth, not to mention any others they could create combined.

What would a human look like "being" a flame, their inner desires never to be tamed? Burning and taking and making their want, and all the while those horrific scars burning their haunts. Flesh melts like wax if its hot enough, leaving it like clay if you keep the stuff. Would it really be that bad, or could it be great? Who knows, but who would dare tempt fate?

What of being of water, the land of the drowned. Where bodies are lest and never are found. The scales on their flesh and the kelp in their hair. Their lungs turned to gills so they could never breath air.

Speaking of such, what would it be to be air? To hear every word, be in every prayer. Would one even need a body in an element like that, for if a mortal flies too high, they're sure to fall flat.

To the earth, our mother, the densest of these. She barely get bothered by the ightiest breeze. Unless, I suppose we speak of the sand, it flies through the air, who knows where it would land. Would they be soil or rock or sand or steel? I suppose that would depend on what they would feel. For the Earth is not one thing, indeed it is many, so variants of this kind would surely be plenty

And most frightful for last, what of a demon? Depends on what you believe in. Are they infinite evil or chaos or freedom? Some may grow fangs, others grow horns, others be shunned for the hate they adorned. Perhaps others would shift and change ever constant, never quite what they were, but quite what they wanted.

Though this is only a story, it is over now, so just remember its lesson, the importance of how a being is not one thing, indeed it is many. So while we all are human, our variations are plenty.

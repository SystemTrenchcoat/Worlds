
Giants were the first draft of mortals, built in the image of [[Kyarus]]. They had bodies appearing as a simpler form of his fractaling being, each branch of [[giant]] taking on a version of his power, all "combining" to be a rough match.

## Aetherial Giants

Blessed with a powerful vibratory sense, they sense the subtle energies and vibrations of the universe.

Aetherial Giants have a completely different understanding of reality than we do. They perceive the deep virations of everything through their very being. Able to see a completely different reality than most creatures, they still navigate the world quite well. They may not have any facial features, but they can feel where they are and what's around them for quite a wats, especially those that train it. They communicate psionically, through emotion and thought rather than word. They are skilled diviners, able to feel the energies of the universe and follow the enrgy of it.

Aetherial Giants are the schemers of the giants, deciding when to take what actions. They are also sent before anyone else to survey new arreas with their vibratory sense and three-fingered that poke into the ground to detect the vibrations through the ground.

## Laiocar Giants

Trained with the ability to widen their awareness far beyond their body, extending their sense of touch. This is only possible thanks to their heightened sense of touch. This is only possible thanks to their heightened sense of touch, they can feel the most subtle movements by touching them. By strengthening and extending their presence, they can feel anything within the radius of their presence. This grants them a powerful blindsight that can tell intimate details if practiced enough.

Laiocar Giants have a lot more nerve endings than any other creature. It their skin apears slightly more textured than one may expect. It is designed to pick up as much stimulus as possible. Their society greatly values collecting and catalogouing. Possibly have fur to help them read energy easier, but through shaving or removing eyes, they can tell more complex things

Laiocar Giants have a movement based language with some possessing minor telepathy. They mostly keep to themselves unless they're out for something related to their collection. They often live in proximaty of Rimecae Giants, trinkets for knowledge and identification. They are skilled at getting aronud and sneaking. Talented ones could grab using psionics.

## Naokar Giants

Created with a powerful sense of smell, they developed a language of spores and pheramones. It is through smell that they interact with much of the world, though through their evolution. The vibrations of the universe can be translated through scent if trained.

Naokar Giants have noses like mini trunks, they go down their face similarly to a long, bushy beard. They have 5 long fingers that slightly probe the ground, enhancing their blindsight to help detect location more precisely. Their sense of smell is powerful enough to determine nearyl anything by its scent. Their toes work similarly to their hands. Their society is built mostly around hunter-gatherers and trackers, the most skilled of [[Eotal]]. (also miners).

Though their fingers are skinny, they are strong, capable of scratching and digging dirt and stone.

Nacokar Giants speak through pheramones and defend with spores. They are also used to mark a target they need to follow. They are often utilized in defense, to track who should be there and who shouldn't. Alternatively, they are amazing trackers of anything with a "smell" to track. Their sense of smell picks up on much more than ours do. Their long nails and fingers can grab intruders or dig through the eo, if kept properly

## Rimecae Giants

Created with the ability to see strands of energy that unite and govern the universe.

Rimecae Giants have larger, almost crystaline looking eyes. They use their abilities of sight to assist the flow of energies through Eotal. They are predominantly academic types, accumulating information and testing theories. They make good magic uses, especially wizards, as well as artificers. They are always testing or researching something and seem to thrive off of simply researching.

Their hair grows up like a tree, changing to match their interest/expertise

Rimecae Giants can learn almost anything with their crystal eyes if harnessed properly. They are information hungry and willing to work with anyone to go on a particular quest for knowledge. They often tend towards logical brained nature, but some have a more feeling focus. They tend to be relatively lawful and abide by the rules and patterns of the universe.

## Casarvae Giants

Gifted in their ability to sense interactions, their ability to taste the environment tells them what's around them.

Casarvae Giants have a taste for the finer things in life. They are able to taste thoughts, emotions, and even ambient energy. They have large mouthes with forked tongues, a small nose, and two small ears, as well as an pair of eyes on either side of their head. They are artistic types by nature, tasting an idea and devoting themselves to creating something that conveys the feeling of it. The more practical ones are among the best smiths.

Casarvae Giants are able to taste much more than their environment. Thoughts, emotions, energy itself. There is so much they're capable of tasting and doing with it. They are often quite talented artist and artisans of all sorts. They are very driven by living their lives of leisure.

## Meizakal Giants

Crafted with the ability to hear between worlds, able to tune into any frequency and hear only from there until it changes its mind. Talented ones can focus on multiple frequencies at once and some ver practiced ones can plane shift on demand with it. They have a deep understanding of language and can hear the root of most sound to determine meaning. They are skilled diplomats, negotiators, and merchants. Very good at hearing what on wants or needs so they can get what they want or need.

Incredibly powerful ones could hear their way into demiplanes and things like that.

Meizakal Giants have keen ears and are practiced in determing languages through their roots in order to translate and learn languages quickly. They are very social most of the time and delight in meeting new people and learning about cultures and interactions. They do a lot of negotiating and peace making, they keep people together.

## Iocan Giants

Being powerfully aware of one's awareness in the universe is a helpful thing. It allows one to adapt easier, to travel across realms like walking.

Iocan Giants are the most like mortals with a roughly similar arrangement of limbs and facial features. They are potently aware of their place in the universe. They are skilled at walking through planes and exploring more generally. They are often cartographers, sailors (eventually), and adventurers. They get the most use out of most tools of the giants.

Opcam Giants cultivate land and lead exploration. They are the first giants to really utilize tools to assist them. They have a decent ability to use all of their sense, if not as powerfully as other giants, but functionally. Their abilties make tracking their location easier, they're said to have a labrinthian recall that could compete with a minotaur's.

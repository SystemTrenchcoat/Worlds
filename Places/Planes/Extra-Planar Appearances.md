## Elemental

The purest form of an element, often impacted by the neigh

### Primary

* [[Magic/Elements/Primary/Eo|Eo]] - A physical structure embodying a law/code

* [[Magic/Elements/Primary/Aer|Aer]] - Clouds/Wisps varying in size and color representing thought

* [[Magic/Elements/Primary/Ner|Ner]] - A flowy depiction of energy (usually waves)

* [[Magic/Elements/Primary/Pyr|Pyr]] - A fiery emanation of a will differing in color and intensity

* [[Magic/Elements/Primary/Tos|Tos]] - A light varying in color, brightness, and segments representing self determination

* [[Magic/Elements/Primary/Brio|Brio]] - A structure branching out like a plant (though oft quite different looking)

* [[Ani|Ani]] - A synaptic web representing experiences

* [[Gia|Gia]] - The embodiment of a creative spark

### Secondary

Paraelementals (including planary included) oft looks like a venn-diagram of their component parts at the intersection

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Order|Order]] - A glowing web forming a spiritual self conception

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Chaos|Chaos]] - Nexus of a synaptic web burning in varying colors

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Magma|Magma]] - The molten form of how will alters a foundation

- [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Mineral|Mineral]] - The physical, organized accumulation of a creative process

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Ooze|Ooze]] - A messy interpretation of creative influence(s)

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Emotion|Emotion]] - A radiant depiction of a complete state of being

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Lightning|Lightning]] - A bright fractal of a train of thought

* [[Temperature|Temperature]] - A cloudy interpretation of passionate thoughts (like steam)

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Sound|Sound]] - A ripple of creative intent in its most complete form (intent taking form that is)

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Flesh|Flesh]] - An organic structure based on a foundation

* [[Blood|Blood]] - A flowing embodiment of a path through existence (like a river)

* [[Dream|Dream]] - An idealized organic embodiment of a life (each branch a perfect/preferred energy)

#### Planary

* [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Planary/Aether|Aether]] - An interpretation of boundless potential

- [[Plasma|Plasma]] - An energetic interpretation of a will

- [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Planary/Glyph|Glyph]] - Symbol(s) that encompass a way of being

- [[Magic/Elements/Secondary/Planary/Spore|Spore]] - A cloud of small organic structures, each varying but related

The hierarchical realm of devils, where the most powerful and cunning rule. Fueled by self-interest, but able to understand how to plan, able to accrue power through subjugation, assimilation, and deals. This realm breeds ambition, and most seek to make everything their property.

The realm of devils, each circle represents something different: Survival, interaction, power, connection, (expression) manifestation, observation, understanding.

It is invoked in spells relating to this power, deals, and contracts

The purest essence of [[Infernis]], a place of sheer desire. Nothing matters more than one's will here. Only one with avarice insatiable is capable of thriving here. Many are created here, though they are swallowed by their betters, resulting in the strong fleeing when possible, terrified of meeting this same drab fate. 

A place of mortal sin, home to those eager to return to the mortal realm for new toys

Used in potent enchantment related to sin and corruption

All serving the one mind, the machines mindlessly march on. Some may witness the bliss of freedom, but here, all know their purpose and serve it. A few may uphold the One Mind's law, though for their power they are reduced to puppets, attempts for the immensely powerful being to understand a life of a separate self.

This plane was a gift from [[Kyarus]] to [[Chryorous]] in an effort to understand life outside itself. He has, however, made a few to keep all of his [Material Realm](Aun%20Materia.md) functioning.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Technosis**. Through research or extraplanar influence, you have learned of powerful, almost futuristic machines. Gain +1d6 on rolls relating to machines.
2. **Technosense**. You have an uncanny ability to track tech. You are aware of any automated machines in the area and can perceive life through their senses should they exist.
3. **Technopath**. **Prereq: Technosense** Your ties to tech grows even further. You have access to two-way communication with any automated tech in the area. They resoid to you with the trust of an authority figure

Home to the most benevolent creatures, it is a near endless paradise. Taking on new forms for its observer, this is what some refer to as heaven. Protected by the strongest of angels, it is an oasis for the weary and kind.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Potent Morality**. +2d4 to rolls involving acts relating to what you consider good and righteous. Subtract this number from rolls that run counter to these ideals
2. **Imposing Morality**. **Prereq: Potent Morality** Creatures that run counter (or commit acts counter) to your vicinity suffer -2d4 to rolls. If this includes you, you may not utilize abilities from this tree.
3. **Purifying Morality**.**Prereq: Imposing Morality** You may purify forces that run counter to your ideals, but cannot change one's heart. The impurity is repelled or removed/destroyed, whichever makes the most sense in the circumstance.

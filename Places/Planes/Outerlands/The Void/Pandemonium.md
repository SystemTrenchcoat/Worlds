The swirling seas of creation that remains after the [infernal realms](Infernis.md) took cohesive shape. Here, all is in eternal flux as all of [[Infernis]] once was. Only one able to manifest through chaos may be able to truly get to their destination through twisting trails transmuting tirelessly.

An ever-changing place of insanity thought to be somehow linked to [[Mageia]]. Home to inter-dimensional chaos creatures.

Used in chaotic transmutation

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Disrupting Monotony**. Gain +2d4 to rolls relating to things you wouldn't usually do (that is still in character), but -1d8 to repeated rolls (resets every scene)
2. **Breaking the Mold**. You may share above effects with a sapient creature you can see (consciously or not). When you do, roll 1d4. 
    1 - Nothing happens
    2 - They feel inclined to act opposite their usual self
    3 - They feel like they have been hollowed out
    4 - They gain a surge of energy.
This effect can be removed by you at will
3. **Altering Energy**. You may disrupt a spell/effect you have seen and make it into a new one you haven't. If you have seen every spell, ability, and effect, create a new one.

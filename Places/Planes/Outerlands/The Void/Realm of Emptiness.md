The purest essence of [the voidal realms](The%20Void.md) one can travel, it is an endless, aetheric vacuum. One only ever sees what was on them when they entered no matter how hard they look, barring those who find their way into the assimilation of [The One Mind](Chryorous.md).

A silent expanse that changes to match the person.

Used to empty full things, end effects, thinking outside self

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Void Heart**. Your emptiness disconnects you from emotion. Suffer -1 die to rolls regarding emotion, but reduce **[Emotional Harms](Harm)** by 1 level.
2. **Void Touched**. **Prereq. Void Heart** The voice beyond calls to you, but you stalwartly refuses its words. Take one effect from the "Void Touched" table
    - Mark of Ancient Knowledge. Memories of times long past flicker through your thoughts and dreams. Gain +2 dice to rolls regarding ancient knowledge and -2 to specific recollections of your life
    - Mark of Disembodied Mind. Your connection to your body grows weaker. Suffer -1 die to rolls regarding your body, but reduce **[Physical Harms](Harm)** by 1.
3. **Void's Curse**. **Prereq. Void Touched** The voice beyond has gotten too loud to ignore. Choose one effect from the "Void's Curse" table
    - Curse of Ultimate Empathy. You hear the souls of those around you in the voices. When you look at a creature, succeed a resist check or take on their emotion and suffer the "Dissolution **[Minor Metaphysical Harm](Harm)**".
    - Curse of Disembodied Soul. You feel a powerful distance between you and your body. You may leave your body and possess another vessel. If it is occupied, roll a contested check.

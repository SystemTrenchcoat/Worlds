Even the strongest of beliefs have power and here they are given form alongside laws of creation. The apex of [[Ceylium]], here, the pen truly is mightier than the sword as philosophy, debate, and spellcraft take presidence above all.

Home to the rest of the angels and virtuous mortal souls, it serves as a battlefield of ideals. Peace and Survival of the Fittest, Free Will and The One, Nature and Civilization, Good and Evil, they all play out here.

A domain of law and order, the heavens are something of an arbiter of peace and judge of morality. Angels are not bound by physicality in the heavens and often appear as they do "biblically," lights with wings covering them. There are other types of celestial that take on more animal forms, such as pegasai, unicorns, and white stags, but these celestials, while often mistaken as being mindless, have as much sentience as any angel. 

It is here where many material animals can trace their roots as this was the first plane to have such creatures. Celestials vary widely in appearance, but all of them have an inherent ||juenal|| sense of right and wrong, right being peace keeping and wrong being chaos bringing. Most act in favor of the benefit of all regardless. After [[Kyarus]], there was a stronger sense of freedom taken on by angels, acting in the will of freedom irrespective to order or chaos, but other angels believe order to be law. There are only good angels in that all prioritize the many over the one, but some take this a very "smite the chaotic" kinda way while others act in more peaceful way.

# The Celestial Realm

Built by [[Sidraous]], the Celestial Realm is a realm of Divine Order. The angels only care about upholding their code, their order, and inflicting this order on any and all who are before them. The vast minority believe in a single idea of what all should be and do, heavily influenced by [[The Void]]. These angels are called the [[airaost]], The Keepers of All Order. They are wildly unpopular among the realms as they believe their ideals trump all others, from the other celestials, to the demons, to the material denizens. They do believe void walkers live in harmony however, so it is believed that they are part of the one mind that connects all under the will of [[Chryorous]].

Other angels in contrast vary wildly in their ideals, their bonds, their code. There are those who believe in the survival of the mighty and spend time training themselves against each other and the more animalistic celestials. There are those who believe in the importance of mindless survival and become these animals, mostly predators. Also, some who believe that the simpler a life, the more easily it can spread its energy, hence unicorns, white stags, etc. There are those who believe in cosmic law and morality and uphold their definitions of it, good, evil, or neutral. So many different creeds of angel, all intent on maintaining order in their own right, but all having an inherent belief about it being the role of order to hold all together.

After [[Kyarus]] came through, new creeds were formed, one's who valued change as a constant of existence, ones who believed in individuality and freedom, ones who believed in the created ideas of what one could be, unbound from such law. The latter became susceptible to falling, creating demonic angels who explored their potential in the depths of [[Xydranous]]'s realm. The former two would be quite ostracized from the more law based angels, seen as chaotic and dangerous by some, and disgustingly disrespected by others. Few angels did believe that one could benefit from having no sense of order or consistency. Individualists were thought to be stripping order away bit by bit and those who welcomed change welcomed chaos in the angels' eyes.

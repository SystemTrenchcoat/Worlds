A blanket of darkness across a realm of sheer consciousness. There are no individuals here, though they are widely echoes of the one known as [[Chryorous]]. They think through his thoughts, see through their perspective, but only as a hive. The fabric of the void also has thought. It decides how it wishes to shape itself, how to best assist the whole of the void, a co-creator of [[Chryorous]], though while [[Chryorous]] governs creatures, they govern lands. 

It is said that the void is formless, though this is not wholly true. The shapes change and twist ever on in the dark, everchanging to suit it. The people change from the presence of other energies interacting upon it, behaving in more contrast to [[Chryorous]], like a prism refracts a slightly different light. The land changes vastly with the creation of new planes. New shapes and forms create piecework of the other realms made using its power. [[Chryorous]] is said to dislike this, though the [[void-walkers]] don't mind.

# The Void Realm

A realm of almost formless space, a mass, a realm of pure potential and consciousness. There are creatures in the void: the [[void-walkers]], traversing the amorphous, light less planes that change to match the whims of a long forgotten entity. The [[void-walkers]] are echoes of [[Chryorous]], the thought given form. The thought given form inhabits the mind of all void-walkers, of all beings who enter its realm, except his brothers of course, too different and powerful in their own rights to fall to his will.

The [[void-walkers]] live their own lives to some degree, able to exist within the idea sent through the thought. However, it is much like a play, everyone given a script of what to do and be, how to exist to meet the needs and desires of [[Chryorous]].

[The brothers](The%20Original%204.md) changed this, each in their own way; [[Xydranous]] incited emotion and will, bursting the limits of their scripts wide open, that they may live their own lives yet. [[Sidraous]] evoked order, a sense of code and law for each to follow, strengthening the script to some degree, forcing law onto people, but his law was different in some degree to [[Chryorous]]'s, allowing some to see a new perspective, to see beyond what was once only instruction. [[Kyarus]] created a desire to grow and change, showing that nothing is static, even in the void. He taught that all have the ability to choose and change their fates.

This all created a fissure in the people from their "god". More and more hungered for a way to see their own lives, be their own rulers, create their own destineys, and over time, they did. Be it through looking into the lives of those on the other realms and emulating them from the void, entering the [[Mynixaceli]], or even through learning how to incarnate elsewhere from the void

A domain of sheer, passionate emotion. Demons are the original inhabitants of the hells, entirely embodying their every whim. Many embody different emotions, both positive and negative, but others embody a purpose that drives their emotions further. The latter would become devils when [[Kyarus]] came through, teaching them by example to harness their purpose in the name of their own power. There is no distinction of good or evil in the hells, it's all about what one desires. This means that while there are war-mongers, tortures, and conquerors, there are also creatives, thrill-seekers, and hunters of purpose or joy. 

The hells have a variety of different denizens and all of them are united in their self-interest. Fiends are always willing to do what has to be done to get what they want, no matter what. This leads to some troubling interactions outside of the hells where more external consideration is valued as well as a stricter sense of morality, at least in [[Ceylium|The Heavens]] and [[Aun Materia|The Material]].

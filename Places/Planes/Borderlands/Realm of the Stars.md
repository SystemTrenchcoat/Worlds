Also known as the astral plane, this is the only path that links all of creation. Through the stars, one can reach beyond the planes to other galaxies, worlds, some say even universes, but truly, no one really knows what lies in the stars.

## TTRPG Abilities

1. **Planar Sight**. See into adjacent planes
2. **Planar Boundries**. **Prereq: Planar Sight** Knowledge of where barrier between is weakest and most traversable
3. **Planar Traversal**. **Prereq: Planar Boundaries** Can walk between planes with or without weak points

## Factions and Politics

### [[Bloodeaters]]

Local [[Bloodeaters|Cult of Hemarrah]], keeps a low profile after blood magic got a bad rep. following [[The Great War]]. Has a presence at local asembly meetings, usually [[Meinicari]] attends to look after interests like a school system teaching divine truths ( god beliefs and powers).

### [[S.E.G.A.A Collective]]

A collective of scientists, alchemists, and artificers of all sorts. Protects the city and equips [[Hidden Serpant|Serpants]] with powerful artillery. Wants to teach artificing and engineering principles to the town in schools.

### [[Children of Nature]]

Nature lovers with an aversion to technology (mechanics) for fear of them clashing against nature (via pollution). Ensures the balance of nature is being kept, usually with regulations and rules about what can't be in a machine by punishment of having tinkering license revoked and/or being stripped of any mechanical equipmenet. For teaching the laws of nature and mechanical regulations in schools. Has a few members and their local leader [[Fenif]] on the assembly.

### Town Guard

A volunteer group of protectors viewed as superfluos by most of the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpants]] ([[Jax]] reminds them they survey the town while the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpants]] deal with real threats). They stand guard around the city and act as the first line of defense against (mostly petty) crime and other threats. They want more funding and better trainging to better serve the town

### [[Illuminators]]

A cult serving [[Tomeria]] and [[Tomeria]] more generally. Dedicated to bringing out the best in all mortals. For an all encompassing school system and often works as the mediary party. Has several members attend the general assembly

Steampunk Brutes
In one of the clockwork towns
Fight Club

Members need to create some sort of weapon or piece of armor and use it to win a round in the ring

Why get in? Membership allows members to win other equipment from the other contestants

When someone wins against an opponent, they earn the right to one piece of equipment they made

In battles, the only equipment allowed has to have been made by the contestant or given by an opponent or they risk expulsion

There is a detailed log catalouging the owners of all equipment in the club

Sale of any equipment obtained or associated with the club is frowned upon

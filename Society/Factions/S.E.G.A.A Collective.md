A faction of the few scientists, engineers, gunsmiths, artilerists, and alchemists of [[Mythica]] and even some [[Leyvie]]. The collevtive was officially start by the [[Orykto Michanos]] twins with a few close colleagues. The guild was created to cultivate the most scientifically and technologically inclined minds in all of [[Eotal]]. This advancement was meant to be for the greater food of all society, however, the [[Children of Nature]] made quick and continuous effort to stunt this growth however possible, fearing that civilization getting too advanced would be the end of natural beauty in [[Mythica]] as they knew it, especially seeing how technology affected [[Leyvie]], leading to the creation of their sister faction, the [[Ambassadors of Nature]], to make sure the state of the country didn't get worse.

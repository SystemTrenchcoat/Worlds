This guild is made up of the finest spies and detectives of the land. The operatives are often hired by other guilds to get information, most notably the [[Justice of the Dragon]], [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive]], and occasionally the [[Hidden Serpent]]. However, they occasionally go to seek truth in rumors and sell of their findings

Something of a sister faction to the [[Hidden Serpant]], the [[Operatives of the Hidden]] are specialists in recon. They do not deal with people like the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpants]], but they are usually spies and detectives, the finest in the land. This guild shares a desire for knowledge with the [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive]] and many members follow the [[Hidden Serpant#Commandments of the Snake]] (these members often work with the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpant]] free of charge). Also, similarly to the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpants]], they are mostly run through payment, which is often high, though differs member to member.

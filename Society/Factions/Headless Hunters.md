
A guild made of and for bounty hunters. The members of this guild come from all walks of life, but they all agree on one thing the power of a gold piece. The bounties on the boards come from all manner of sources from [[Erimos]]'s [[Hidden Serpant|Serpants]] to the dark operatives of the [[Syndicate of Shadows|Syndicate]]. The members are welcome to take or ignore any bounty, however they are strictly, forbidden to interfere with bounties they did not take. Additionally, this guild is one of the few that have little to no interactions with the [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive]]. Due to the slightly less than legal nature of the organization, interaction with the knowledge obsessed [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive|Lyceum]] is frowned upon. There are some secrets within the guild the higher ups would rather keep secrets.

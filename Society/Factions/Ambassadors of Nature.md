This guild isn't very active in [[Mythica]], but is much more active in [[Leyvie]]. This guild is full of people who want a union between nature and civilization with minimal inconvenience of either. They make sure that neither try to destroy each other

A less extreme sister-faction to the [[Children of Nature]], the [[Ambassadors of Nature|Ambassadors]] are a faction attempting to make sure that nature is respected an maintained. [[Leyvie]] has had an industrial revolution or three and as a result, is much more advanced than [[Mythica]]. The [[Ambassadors of Nature|Ambassadors]] make sure that the environment does not suffer for the greed of civilization on the whole.

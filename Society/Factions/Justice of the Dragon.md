Contrary to what the name may suggest, this guild was not meant to be made of or for dragons. This guild is meant to serve as law enforcement. Members of this guild are generally city guards, but there are a few that look into missing persons cases and murders. Jobs from this guild often entail tracking down and bringing criminals or giving info on criminals.

Named after its founder, [[Vultras Caerxiros]]. [[Vultras Caerxiros|Vultras]] was a feared and respected general during the [[Mythican Civil War]] where he got the moniker "[[Vultras Caerxiros|The Dragon]]". [[Vultras Caerxiros|Vultras]] had been known as a very virtuous person, so after the war, he used this reputation to create an organization to uphold justice, thus giving rise to the "[[Justice of the Dragon]]" Guild whose primary focus is keeping [[Mythica]] safe from harm and corruption. The use of the founder's namesake gave the guild the prestige and fame to quickly got the guild a lot of supporters and members who truly agree with the guild's goals. The guild works closely with the [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive]] to get recon on both members and enemies. The first is the most important; in order for the guild to accomplish its goals, there can not be corruption in its midst.

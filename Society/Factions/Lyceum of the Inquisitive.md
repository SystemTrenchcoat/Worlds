This guild is devoted to the pursuit of knowledge. Most members would give anything to find a rare book, document, or a rare organism. While some of the members don't practice magic, many members do, in fact, most of them are straight out of the [[Academy of Mystic Arts]]. This guild simply seeks knowledge for all of its members.

Started by the curious gnome [[Kelryn Palehide]], the [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive]] is a guild fueled by curiousity and a general desire for knowledge. While some guilds, such as the [[Hidden Serpant]], are driven by things like money, the [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive|Lyceum]]  is driven by knowledge "information for information" as they often say. They rarely charge money for their services, but do require some sort of exchange of knowledge, be it a rare book or a rumor

A group that adores and worships nature above all else. The members of this guild will protect nature from being destroyed and is the biggest reason [[Mythica]] isn't fully industrial

Started by [[Linnea of the Fae]], The [[Children of Nature]] is a group devoted to keeping the natural order. The goal of the [[Children of Nature|Children]] is to make sure civilization does not get advanced to the point of disturbing nature. The [[Children of Nature|Children]] monitor things such as that the environment is not in danger. The [[Children of Nature|Children]] are generally at odds with the [[Naturae Summa]] and both the [[Hidden Serpant]] and the [[Justice of the Dragon]] as neither see the actions of the [[Children of Nature|Children]] as good. However, the [[Children of Nature|Children]] do get along with the [[Lyceum of the Inquisitive]] as they occasionally supply useful information that help the [[Children of Nature|Children]] maingain the balance.

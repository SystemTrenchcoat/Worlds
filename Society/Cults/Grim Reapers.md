The holy warriors of [[Eido]], God of Death. Almost all of the members are reborn through [[Eido]]'s power. These warriors mostly hunt undead, though a few hunt on their own moral compass.

The predominantly undead force of [[Eido]]. Despite loathing the undead, [[Eido]] has been known to make pacts with souls who he thinks can serve him. It is said that the [[Grim Reapers]] have existed since the beginning of time, but they unsurprisingly fell dormant when the gods disappeared. The first of the new reapers, [[Dulciendium Irae]], is the current "Death", Eido's right hand, as [[Eido]] did not restore the previous reapers to their former status. While many have since moved on, others have not, turning to [[Necrotori]] in an act of resentment.

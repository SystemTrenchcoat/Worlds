This assassin's guild isn't your run of the mill assassin's guild. This guild hunts down criminals and brings them to justice, as well as steal from thieves to give victims back their belongings.

A cult of sorts worshiping [[Erimos]]. Currently headed by [[Jax]]. While similar to the [[Justice of the Dragon]] guild, this group isn't nearly as virtuos. While the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpant]] does aim to do good, its members are not disuaded from commiting... morally dubious acts. The assassins are allowed to take most contracts regardless of law. In addition, while they do help, it is for pay, leaving them suseptible to corruption. Some people prefer to deal with the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpant]] than the [[Justice of the Dragon|Dragon]] because of their hasty solution of killing said problem as opposed to the [[Justice of the Dragon|dragon]]'s who capture it. While the [[Hidden Serpant|Serpant]] is ironically well known, its methods are not universally accepted, hence another reason many people prefer dealing with the [[Justice of the Dragon|Dragon]] instead.

## Commandments of the Snake

The list of rules that one must follow to be a part of the guild written by [[Erimos]] himself.

- Secrets among brothers go to the grave
- While lying to outsiders is expected, brothers are always to be truthful to each other
- Brothers are bound before ~~and past death~~ and are to protect each other until the bitter end
- Brothers above all

## Abilities

**Latent Charge**. ==For the purposes of **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skills/Magic/Enchantment/Enchantment|Enchantment]]** and **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skills/Magic/Clockwork/Clockwork|Clockwork]]**, you may power a contraption using this crystal. When a contraption is powered in this way, it rolls with a dice pool of 3d6 to produce its effect. When used in any other type of casting, it can add 3d6 to the user's dice pool. The effect must make sense with the energies associated with the crystal to use either ability==

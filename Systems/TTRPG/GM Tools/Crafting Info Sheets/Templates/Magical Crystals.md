## Abilities

**Latent Charge**. ==For the purposes of **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skills/Magic/Enchantment/Enchantment|Enchantment]]** and **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skills/Magic/Clockwork/Clockwork|Clockwork]]**, you may power a contraption using this crystal. When a contraption or enchantment is powered in this way, it rolls with a dice pool of 3d6 to produce its effect, increasing by 1d6 per **[[Level of Success]]**. The charges of the enchantment or contraption still have the same max and replenishment rate as when it was created. When used in any other type of casting, it can add 3d6 to the user's dice pool. The effect must make sense with the energies associated with the crystal to use either ability. The dice pool may increase or decrease with size/weight and clarity==

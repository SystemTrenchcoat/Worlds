
Normally, you look like some other mortal, but you may transform into a humanoid beast (and must on a full moon). ==Once a day, you may transform into your beast form, selecting 3 [[Adaptation.canvas]] leaves that fit with said form (must be selected at creation and can not be changed). Roll a Will check when you attempt to enter or leave this form. On a fail, you must follow your beast's instinct until the next dawn. If you attempt to transform more than once a day, you lose 1 die on the Will check and gain the "Consumed" **[Moderate Metaphysical Harm](Harm)**==. What is the origin of your curse? How do you feel about it? Most importantly, what is your "beast's instinct"? While the former 2 are helpful for getting to know your character, the latter *must* be discussed with your GM at the earliest possible opportunity.


You have learned to more efficiently power your contraptions. ==Choose a power. As an action, you or any other creature can boost the efficacy of your contraption by adding what they would roll in a **Will** or **Spellcraft** check for that power. This can also be used to revitalize a contraption that was previously disabled, utilizing the aforementioned **[[Dice Pool]]** for effects instead of your **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skill Trees/Magics/Clockwork|Clockwork]]** **[[Dice Pool]]**


Spells are cast as long performances, from chanting to dance, to anything else really, gaining strength as they are performed.
==You perform rituals that produce magical effects as long as they are being performed. To do so, take a **[[Spellcast]] [[Actions|Action]]** and it is rolled as necessary (if you're generating a field of light or protection, your **[[Narrator]]** may have you take a **[[Resist]] [[Actions|Action]]** to see if you are able to keep it up/how well). You may cast 1 spell per rest==

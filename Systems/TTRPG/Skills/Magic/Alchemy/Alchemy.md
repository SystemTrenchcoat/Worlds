
Prepared potions made as potent as the practitioner can manage, but decay over time. ==You cast spells through brewing potions. To do so, you may take a **Long [[Tinker]]** or **[[Spellcast]] [[Actions|Action]]** to brew a potion. They start off with a **[[Dice Pool]]** equal to your **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skills/Magic/Alchemy/Alchemy|Alchemy]] [[Level]]** and decrease by 1 every day at dawn. When a potion reaches dawn with 0 dice, it becomes inert. Potions may only be affected by 2 **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skill Trees/Magics/Alchemy|Alchemy]]** skills at a time==

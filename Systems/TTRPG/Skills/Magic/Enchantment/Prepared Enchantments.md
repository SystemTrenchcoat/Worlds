==You can take a **Long [[Tinker]]** or **[[Spellcast]] [[Actions|Action]]** to create **Prepared Enchantments**. **Prepared Enchantments** roll one **Spellcraft** die higher. Prepared enchantments have a **Spellcraft Roll** charges and regain a **Spellcraft Roll** charges to a max of your **Spellcraft [[Die Max]]** each dawn
Creatures can attempt to use the item past that, but it uses their stats (usually **Spellcraft**) as appropriate. Additionally, the caster has enchantment slots equal to their **Spellcraft [[Die Max]]==


You can enchant items with your magic.

==You can make temporary magic items. These items have 1 charge and have **Spellcraft** dice equal to the number of skills obtained in this branch. You may make 1 of these at a time.==

## Removing Enchantments

==If you would exceed your max enchantment slots, you may choose to remove the magic from an enchantment you have made. You, or another creature may utilize it by rolling **Spellcraft** instead of your **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skill Trees/Magics/Enchantment|Enchantment]]**

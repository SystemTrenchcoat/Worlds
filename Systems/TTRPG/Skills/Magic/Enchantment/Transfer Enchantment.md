
==You can remove one enchantment of yours within line of sight and place it on a non-magical item. The original item acts as described for **[Removing Enchantments](Systems/TTRPG/Skills/Magic/Enchantment/Enchantment#Removing Enchantments|Enchantment)**. If the enchantment is removed from the non-magical item, the item will return to being non-magical (and does not behave as described for removing enchantments)==

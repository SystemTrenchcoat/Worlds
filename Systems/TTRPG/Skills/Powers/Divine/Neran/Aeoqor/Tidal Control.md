
Your connection to the tide allows you some control over it/ ==You can manipulate the flow of waterways in sight, changing their flow, parting them, or even causing tidal waves. Furthermore, you may shift the tide, gaining twice the bonus dice and an ability that occurs on use:==
- ==**Whelm** (Entering high tide). Whether you succeed or fail on your next roll, all eyes are on you==
- ==**Wash** (Entering low tide). You may choose to wash away 1 non-physical, non-spell effect affecting those in sight (usually a feeling or similar)==
==You may use this ability **3 times a day** and push beyond this limit by making a **[[Harness]] [[Actions|Action]]**, or by taking the "Drained" **[[Harm|Minor Metaphysical Harm]]**==


You have learned one of the secrets to a devil's power. ==You can make deals with creation itself, swearing to do or give something for greater power. Your only limits are your imagination and the **[[Narrator|Narrator's]]** approval, but the **"Inner Deals"** Chart lists some examples. That said, these deals come with a price equal to the power (some examples are included in the **"Inner Cost"** Chart) and dire consequences (some listed on the **"Deal Gone Wrong"** Chart). The costs and consequences should be relative to the benefit and the consequences for breaking these deals should be great, ideally befitting the offense.

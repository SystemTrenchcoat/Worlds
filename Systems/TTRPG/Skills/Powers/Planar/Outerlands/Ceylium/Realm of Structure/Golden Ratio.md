
You understand how the relationships between things can be altered. ==You may make a **[[Harness]]** action to alter the ratios between things to gain +1 die (**Practical**) or +1 die size (**Aesthetic**). (**Practical** changes proportions of a whole, while **Aesthetic** changes the proportions of a part) You must describe how these relationships are changed to gain this effect (ex. I make my grip wider, but sword a bit shorter to make it easier to control (**Practical**),  I make my grip wider and shorter to make it easier to control (**Aesthetic**) ). This effect lasts until you leave the presence of the affected==.

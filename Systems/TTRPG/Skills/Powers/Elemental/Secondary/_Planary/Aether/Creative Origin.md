
==You may temporarily create an object you are familiar with from thought alone. To do so, take a Difficulty 13 **[[Harness]]** action, increasing depending in increments of 6 based on complexity and familiarity. You may perform this act once per **[[Systems/TTRPG/Skill Trees/Powers/Elemental/Secondary/Planary/Aether|Aether]] [[Skill Level]]**. If you push beyond this, suffer the "Drained" **Moderate Physical [[Harm]]**. It must be a non-magical item that can fit within a 5' cube==

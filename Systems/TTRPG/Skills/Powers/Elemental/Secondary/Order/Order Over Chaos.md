
==Once a rest/day, you may take a **[[Harness]] [[Actions|Action]]** to bring a scene to your definition of order. The **Difficulty** increases with the number of moving parts, including, and especially, conscious/thinking creatures that would resist. You may push beyond this limit, but suffer the "Drained" **[[Harm|Moderate Metaphysical Harm]]** when you do so. Additionally you may take a Difficulty 24 **[[Harness]] [[Actions|Action]]** when you would instead roll on a random table. On a success, you choose the result. On a fail, the **[[Narrator]]** chooses the result however they see fit==.

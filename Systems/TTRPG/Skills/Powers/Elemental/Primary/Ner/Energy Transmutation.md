
You have learned the secret of transforming one energy into another. ==You may allow a creature to reallocate a number of points equal to a single die **Spellcraft** or **Will** roll in their power skill trees. It may move across skill trees. 
You may also attempt to pull another's energy to you, weakening them while bolsetering yourself. Roll a **Spellcraft** or **Will** check with a starting **Difficulty** of 13, increasing by 3 with each subsequent use. If you succeed, you steal a power you are aware they possess. This removes the highest level ability in that tree. If there are multiple, the **[[Narrator]]** decides. This point may be used by anyone you choose. On a fail, they are immune to this ability for a full day. 
You may use each of these abilities once a day, suffering the "Drained" **[[Harm|Moderate Metaphysical Harm]]** if you push through this limit.==

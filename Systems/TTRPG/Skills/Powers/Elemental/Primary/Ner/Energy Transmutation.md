
==You have learned the secret of transforming one energy into another. You may allow a creature to reallocate a point in their power skill trees. It may move across skill trees. You may also attempt to pull another's energy to you, weakening them while bolsetering yourself. Roll opposing Magic checks. If you are the victor, you steal a point from a power you are aware they possess. This removes the highest level ability in that tree. If there are multiple, the GM decides. This point may be used by anyone you choose. On a fail, they are immune to this ability for a full day.==

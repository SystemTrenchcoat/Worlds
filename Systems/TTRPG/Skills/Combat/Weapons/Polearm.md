
With their length, they easily allow warriors to keep their distance, and give skilled fighters the ability to control the battlefield

## Maneuvers

- **Sweep**. You utilize the length of your weapon to topple your opponent. ==The target is knocked over. All rolls against them are made with an additional die==

## Styles

- **Polearm Master**. You are practiced in the art of the polearm. ==Gain +1 die to keeping your distance with a creature in combat==

## Stances

- **Shepard**. You are practiced in moving others around the battlefield as you see fit. ==You may move an opponent while you are attacking. They will not knowingly move into an apparent hazard==
- **Rush**. ==You may do two maneuvers during your turn instead of just one==

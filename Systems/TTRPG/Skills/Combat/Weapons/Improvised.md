
To be able to fight with whatever presents itself is a feat of great skill, or great barbarity, depending on who you ask.

## Maneuvers

- **Shatter**. You can break your makeshift weapon, against your opponent disorienting them. ==You may break your weapon against your opponent dealing the "Disoriented" Moderate Physical Harm. Gain +1 die on a roll pertaining to the target over the next round==

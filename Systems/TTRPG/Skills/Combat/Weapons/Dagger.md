
A small blade, easily capable of dealing light damage, but can be directed to strike people where they are weakest

## Maneuvers

- **Victimize**. You strike the enemy at their vital points. ==Deal the "Crippled" Light Physical Harm to the enemy. You and allies gain +1 die against the enemy==

## Style

- **Shinobi**. You are adept at moving stealthily and striking your opponent's weaknesses. ==Gain +1 die on "called shots"==

## Stances

- **Obscure**. ==Gain +1 die on rolls relating to hiding==
- **Assault**. ==Gain +1 die to rolls utilizing the environment to attack==


A versatile weapon used to strike multiple foes in a single movement, knock foes over, or simply striking hard and fast, usually non-lethally

## Maneuvers

- **Whack**. You strike the opponent with a blow that leaves them scatterbrained. ==Deal the "Disoriented" Minor Physical Harm. Gain +1 die on a roll pertaining to the target over the next round==
 - **Sweep**. You utilize the length of your weapon to topple your opponent. ==The target is knocked over. All rolls against them are made with an additional die==


Your origin is how your form came to be what it is. It is usually the species of someone, but on occassion, people undergo changes later in life that would become their "origin". Origins often focus on [[Adaptation|Adaptations]] or sometimes [[Full Powers List|Powers]], sometimes, they may include learned skills and traits. Your origin should predominantly be tied to your family, town, or anything else that deeply ties you to who you are, beyond a mere profession. Bare this in mind while you allocate your **Origin Points**, whether you utilize the following presets or not.

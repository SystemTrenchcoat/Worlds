
- Tinker - Roll **Deftness** or **Diligence** to make something
- Interface - Roll something applicable to the goal. On a success, diminish the **[[Result]]**
- Search - Roll **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Detect** to find something
- Traverse - Roll (probably) **Agility** to get through a place
- Help - Add 1 applicanle die to another player's pool
- Setup - Increase **[[Result]]**/**[[Stance]]** of a specified future roll by 1
- Attack - Roll **Prowess**/**Power** to bioently solve a problem
- Defend - Roll **Power**, **Prowess**, or **Agility** to decrease incoming harm by 1 level
- Heal - Recover 1 level of **[[Harm]]**

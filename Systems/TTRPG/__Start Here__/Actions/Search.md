
Roll **Recall**, **Diligence**, or **Discern** to find something.
- **Recall** for utilizing learned knowledge
- **Diligence** for doing research or other hands on searching
- **Discern** for finding differences, especially in people

## Quick

==On a failure, you don't find it==

## Long

==On a failure, it takes a while to find it (assuming it is there)==


Roll something applicable to the goal to increase the result. A future roll can only have 1 **Setup [[Actions|Action]]** associated with it (success or fail) unless specified otherwise by the **[[Narrator]]**. Subsequent **Setup [[Actions]]** should differ from the initial one and should likely be done by seperate players.

## Quick

==On a success, increase the result by 1 **[[Level of Success]]**==

## Long

==On a success, increase the result by 2 **[[Level of Success|Levels of Success]]**==


Roll **Agility** or **Recall** to get through a place
- **Agility** for physical maneuvers
- **Recall** for utilizing past knowledge (such as remembering if there was another way around)

## Short

On failure, you fail to get around, resulting in [[Harm|Minor Harm]] in a lot of cases

## Long

It takes a while to find your way to wherever your going, but you make it... eventually


## Suffering from [[Harm]]

- Have you had harm invoked?
- Have you had long term harm invoked?
- Have you gained long term harm?

## Collabrative Roleplaying

- Have you meaningfully engaged with your party?
- You contributed meaningfully to another player's story?
- Your party had a cool collective moment?

## Personal Roleplaying

- Have you engaged with a flaw?
- Have you engaged with your past?
- Have you done something cool?
- Have you faced a fear?
- Have you showcased your strengths?
- Have you faced your weaknesses?
- You have rolled an "Impossible" check (that makes sense in cannon and the GM approves of)

## Goal Progression

- Have you furthered a long term goal?
- Have you completed a long term goal?
- Have you gained a new long term goal?
- Have you completed an arc (changed as a person)?
- You have worked towards a group goal?


At the end of each session, the **[[Narrator]]** should hand out at least 4 points to each player. To obtain these points, the **[[Narrator]]** should consider the following questions, however, it is the players' job to sing their praises to outline why and how they have earned these skill points.

## Suffering from [[Harm]]

- Have you had harm invoked?
- Have you had long term harm invoked?
- Have you gained long term harm?

## Collaborative Roleplaying

- Have you meaningfully engaged with your party?
- Have you contributed meaningfully to another player's story?
- Has your party had a cool collective moment?

## Personal Roleplaying

- Have you engaged with a flaw?
- Have you engaged with your past?
- Have you done something cool?
- Have you faced a fear?
- Have you showcased your strengths?
- Have you faced your weaknesses?
- Have you rolled an "Impossible" check (that makes sense in cannon and the **[[Narrator]]** approves of)

## Goal Progression

- Have you furthered a long term goal?
- Have you completed a long term goal?
- Have you gained a new long term goal?
- Have you completed an arc (changed as a person)?
- Have you worked towards a group goal?

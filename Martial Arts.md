- **Stun**. You strike the opponent in such a way, they are unable to move for but a moment. ==Deal the "Stunned" Light Physical Harm to the opponent. They are unable to move until the end of their next turn. A creature may only suffer this effect every other round==

- **Gale**. You send a gust of wind from your wings that sends projectiles off course. ==You may force a projectile off its mark in response to one being fired==

### Styles

- **Flying Warrior**. Prereq. **Wings**. Your feathers are hardened for offense and defense. ==You may use you may perform the **Fire**, **Attack**, and **Parry** actions with your wings.==
